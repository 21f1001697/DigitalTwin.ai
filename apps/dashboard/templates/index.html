Why this comparison is the point, not a footnote

Both stations received the identical {{ severity_pct }}% cycle-time and vibration-proxy drift. The instrumented station's clean signal clears the detection threshold quickly and with high confidence. The manual-checklist station's noisier, indirectly-reconstructed signal may sit below threshold at the same severity — the twin stays honest about that gap instead of hiding it, and this is exactly the station the proposal's ranked instrumentation shortlist would prioritise for a low-cost vision upgrade.
